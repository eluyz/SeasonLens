Wheat and EUR/PLN: from a workbook to a report

Import only wheat and EUR/PLN from the same workbook, check the PLN-per-tonne conversion, understand the short history and save a report. Allow roughly 5–10 minutes on a desktop; mapping columns can take longer on a phone.

All observations are invented. They are not MATIF closes, central-bank rates or a forecast. No account or installation is needed.

1. Open the example file

Start in a fresh demo tab with six built-in samples. Expand Start here — explore, import or create a report and choose Download example XLSX template, or use the download above.

Choose Open private import. Select seasonlens-import-template.xlsx under Excel workbook or CSV file, set Analysis cutoff to 2026-10-06, then choose Open and preview file. Opening the file does not import it.

3. Validate, review, then add

Choose Validate selected instruments. Check two instruments, each with 210 observations, first date 2025-12-17, last date 2026-10-06, 0 blank values skipped and 0 future rows excluded.

Row 3 is outside the physical data range, so it is not counted as a future-row omission. Nothing has been added yet.

Choose Add validated instruments to this tab once. There should now be eight instrument entries: six built-in samples and two private imports. Imported examples have USER_FILE provenance; this describes the import, not an official data provider. Changing an import setting requires validation again.

4. Check wheat in PLN per tonne

Open Analysis options. Choose Wheat sample · private Excel under Instrument, then PLN/t under Display units. Select the private import, not built-in wheat.

Checkpoint: cutoff 2026-10-06; 210 observations; last observation 2026-10-06; conversion note shows 210 exact-date positive pairs and no unconverted commodity observations.

Only the declared EUR/PLN column from this import is used. No sample FX is substituted and no rate is carried forward. The last historical workbook row, 213, gives this independent check:

213.29 EUR/t × 4.2051 PLN per EUR
= 896.905779 PLN/t ≈ 896.91 PLN/t

For 100 tonnes that is 89,690.58 PLN. The whole-unit daily reference beside Monthly average prices displays 897. For full precision choose Export selected observations (CSV); its final date is 2026-10-06 and value is approximately 896.905779 (binary floating-point export can show 896.9057789999999). Technical indicator columns are also included.

5. Read the charts without filling the gaps

Use View monthly averages and View five-year prices in Start here. Monthly averages in PLN/t average the daily converted products; they do not multiply separate monthly wheat and FX averages. Commodity tables show whole units while calculations keep their precision. Colors compare unrounded monthly means with the last daily price; they are not trading signals.

Monthly average prices — last five years including this year keeps 2022–2026. Years 2022–2024 and January–November 2025 lack observations; November–December 2026 are after the cutoff. Dashes and gaps mean unavailable data, not zero. The dashed period average weights available yearly monthly means equally. October 2026 is partial with four observations. This short history cannot establish a reliable seasonal pattern.

For Technical analysis — last 12 months, retain Daily chart range: Last 12 months and all six checkboxes: price, SMA20, SMA100, SMA200 and two Bollinger bands. The supplied history is shorter than 12 months. The first 19 observations lack SMA20/Bollinger, the first 99 lack SMA100 and the first 199 lack SMA200: only 11 SMA200 values exist. Indicators require full observation windows, not calendar-day or exchange-session guarantees. Earlier supplied history warms them before the displayed range; missing history is never fabricated.

6. Check historical risk and coverage

Under Historical risk and statistical analysis, open Historical risk and stress episodes. Keep these settings:

The history has 209 one-interval changes. At 95%, the equivalent tail size is 10.45 observations, above the minimum of five. Change Tail confidence to 99%: only 2.09 tail observations remain and VaR/Expected Shortfall become unavailable. This is a coverage safeguard. Switch back to 95% before the report.

A buyer treats rises as adverse; a seller treats falls as adverse. The budget scenario applies historical percentage changes to the current benchmark and quantity. It is not transaction profit/loss or a prediction. The five-year setting requests a window; it does not make this short workbook a five-year dataset.

Optionally open Commodity and FX contributions to PLN price risk: there are 209 exact matching intervals. Its variance shares include covariance and are not proof of causation or hedge effectiveness. Completed-year seasonality requires more history; this template cannot support its historical-mean uncertainty intervals.

7. Build and save the report

Keep the private wheat, PLN/t, cutoff 2026-10-06 and the risk settings above. Choose Open report builder in Start here. Retain the four initially checked sections:

  • Market snapshot and data coverage
  • Monthly average prices and five-year comparison
  • Technical chart with selected lines
  • Historical risk and stress episodes

Choose Build report preview. Check Wheat sample, PLN/t, 2026-10-06 and USER_FILE in the metadata.

Download HTML saves a standalone report with selected results and charts. Print / Save PDF uses your browser's print dialog; choose Save as PDF if offered. PDF saving, pagination and mobile support depend on the browser; HTML is the fallback. The snapshot stays fixed. Choose Back to analysis and rebuild after changing settings.

Reports omit the original observation arrays and application code. Visible tables, prices and declared sources can still be private; sharing a report shares those results.

Finish and known limits

Save results before reloading. Reload removes both private imports and restores six built-in samples; browser imports are not stored in SQLite. Display preferences may persist, imported observations do not. Use the local app for persistent private history.

There is no live-data collection or automatic refresh in this exercise. Its dates are invented weekdays, not a checked exchange calendar. It does not establish predictive skill, five-year coverage or real-market suitability. Wide tables/charts scroll inside their frames; on a keyboard focus the frame and use arrow keys. Mapping is easier on a larger screen. Physical-phone, Safari and native PDF-dialog behavior remain platform checks.
